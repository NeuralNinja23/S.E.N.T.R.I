SENTRI Change Log
V2.2.0 – Conversation Intelligence

Status: Planned
Architecture: In Development

Objective

Transform Sentri from a response-generation system into a conversational digital human by enabling it to understand, plan, and execute conversations intelligently before reasoning begins.

Conversation Intelligence becomes the decision-making layer responsible for determining how Sentri should continue a conversation, rather than simply generating the next response.

Design Principle

Conversation Intelligence does not generate responses.

It decides how the conversation should proceed before Memory, Reasoning, and Tools are invoked.

This makes Conversation Intelligence the cognitive layer that transforms Sentri from a language model interface into a conversational digital human.
